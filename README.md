# Eco Journey

أكيد، ده Prompt جاهز لـ Lovable، مبني على شكل الـ layout اللي في الصورة وعلى الـ content اللي بعتيه، مع الحركة والـ navigation:

Create a premium, cinematic, interactive single-page landing website for “BlueGreen”.



The website should follow the visual structure and layout shown in the provided reference image. Keep the design elegant, editorial, environmental and immersive — not like a generic corporate sustainability website.



CORE CONCEPT:

BlueGreen connects people with the environment through an educational “journey” based on play, ecological thinking, music, transformation and storytelling.



The website should feel like scrolling through an environmental story/journey rather than simply reading a normal website.



VISUAL DIRECTION:

- Premium environmental / editorial aesthetic

- Cinematic nature photography

- Deep forest greens, muted olive greens, soft cream/off-white

- Use natural textures: forests, plants, water, soil, trees, landscapes

- Combine realistic photography with delicate hand-drawn outline illustrations

- Outline illustrations should be thin, elegant and organic

- Main outline color: soft white / warm off-white

- Avoid excessive graphic elements

- Lots of breathing space

- Sophisticated typography

- Minimal, clean navigation

- No gradients that look artificial

- No stock-looking corporate graphics

- The overall feeling should be calm, intelligent, creative and immersive



IMAGE USAGE:

Use the provided environmental images as the main visual assets for the sections.



The image sequence should feel connected:

1. Hero — cinematic green landscape / sea and nature landscape

2. Environmental Journey — natural environmental image

3. Play Sustainability — lush green forest / plants

4. Eco Logic — close-up ecosystem / plant and soil image with ecological outline drawings

5. Eco Melody — forest / stream environment with musical visual language

6. Waste Wonder — upcycling / natural materials / transformed objects image

7. Planet Story — forest / nature storytelling image with hand-drawn illustrations

8. Mind Map — the illustrated planet mind-map concept

9. Contacts — clean cream section



Do NOT replace the supplied visual direction with generic stock images.



NAVIGATION:

Create a fixed minimal navigation bar at the top.



Left:

BlueGreen logo.



Right:

- The Journey

- Play

- Logic

- Melody

- Wonder

- Story



The navigation should remain minimal and elegant while scrolling.



IMPORTANT LOGO INTERACTION:

When the user clicks/taps the BlueGreen logo on the LEFT side of the navigation, smoothly scroll to the dedicated MIND MAP section at the bottom of the website.



The logo should therefore act as a navigation trigger to the Mind Map section.



Use smooth scrolling with a subtle cinematic transition.



PAGE STRUCTURE:



01 — HERO



Full-screen cinematic environmental image.



Place the BlueGreen logo in the navigation.



Main slogan:

“Transforming our future together”



Keep the hero typography minimal.



Add a subtle scroll indicator at the bottom.



Animation:

- Very subtle slow image movement / parallax

- Slight natural movement while scrolling

- Text should fade in gently

- Nothing flashy





02 — ENVIRONMENTAL JOURNEY



Create a large editorial section inspired by the provided reference layout.



Use a split or spacious composition with a natural image and cream background.



Title:

“Environmental Journey”



Subtitle:

“Learning that transforms knowledge into new ways of thinking, acting, and living.”



Body:

“BlueGreen edutainment journey explores different environmental aspects, from climate change to the circular economy, mapping different layers of intervention that help transform the future, build resilience, and reveal sustainable solutions.



This journey is more than learning; it’s a fun, game-changing experience. Together we play, we sing, we upcycle, and we tell the planet’s story in ways that inspire creativity and responsibility.”



Use subtle organic line-art around the image.



Animation:

- Image slowly reveals as the user scrolls

- Text fades and moves upward slightly

- Hand-drawn outlines appear progressively





03 — PLAY SUSTAINABILITY



Use a cinematic lush green forest image.



Title:

“Play Sustainability”



Text:

“Games and playful challenges open doors to new ways of seeing the planet. Through imagination and interaction, sustainability becomes a joyful path of discovery and action.”



Create a strong visual section with the image taking approximately half to two-thirds of the screen.



Add the “Sticky Man” style environmental outline illustration language.



Use simple animated outline elements.



Animation:

- Small outline elements gently move / draw themselves

- Very subtle floating movement

- Image has slow parallax





04 — ECO LOGIC



Use the close-up ecosystem image with plants, soil and water.



Title:

“Eco Logic”



Text:

“Activities unfold like pieces of a puzzle, building logic and advancing perception of the environment. Connections and interrelations emerge, revealing the ecosystem as a living, dynamic whole.”



Add delicate white hand-drawn ecological diagrams over the image:

- water drops

- sun

- roots

- leaves

- circular connections

- small ecosystem symbols



The line drawings should feel hand-drawn and organic, not like technical UI icons.



Animation:

As the user scrolls, the ecological lines and symbols appear progressively and connect to each other.





05 — ECO MELODY



Use the cinematic forest/stream image.



Title:

“Eco Melody”



Text:

“Nature’s sounds become melodies that invite listening and respect. Singing for and about the planet turns its message into harmony, echoing our commitment to a transformed future.”



Visual concept:

A group is NOT required in the image.



Prefer nature-only imagery.



Overlay elegant white line-art:

- musical notes

- sound waves

- leaves

- flowing organic lines



The musical lines should visually flow through the natural environment.



Animation:

Sound-wave lines subtly move or pulse.

Musical notes can gently appear/disappear while scrolling.



Keep it sophisticated and minimal.





06 — WASTE WONDER



Use the provided upcycling / transformed natural objects image.



Title:

“Waste Wonder”



Text:

“What was once discarded is reborn as something useful and beautiful. Transformation reveals the hidden value of materials, honoring resources by turning waste into wonder.”



Layout:

Use the same editorial split-screen visual language shown in the reference.



One side:

Cream background with typography.



Other side:

Large natural upcycling image.



Add subtle circular/recycling-inspired hand-drawn lines.



Animation:

- Image reveal

- Slow zoom

- Circular outline animation





07 — PLANET STORY



Use a cinematic forest/nature image.



Title:

“Planet Story”



Text:

“Narratives unfold not only from history but also from personal and imaginative voices. Together they weave a shared vision, telling the planet’s story toward a transformed and hopeful future.”



Add the hand-drawn environmental “Sticky Man” illustration language.



Possible outline illustrations:

- person reading

- planet

- tree

- leaves

- storytelling / speech bubbles

- connected people

- recycling

- planting



The illustrations should be white thin outlines placed directly over the image.



Animation:

The illustrations should draw themselves subtly as the user reaches the section.





08 — MIND MAP



Create a dedicated full-width Mind Map section.



This section is connected to the BlueGreen logo interaction.



When the user clicks the BlueGreen logo from anywhere on the website, smoothly scroll here.



Central concept:

A hand-drawn PLANET / EARTH outline instead of a brain shape.



The planet should act as the center of the mind map.



Around the planet, create connected branches representing the BlueGreen journey:

- Play Sustainability

- Eco Logic

- Eco Melody

- Waste Wonder

- Planet Story

- Environmental Journey



Use a hand-drawn infographic style.



The central planet should have:

- hand-drawn Earth outline

- subtle orbit lines

- organic sketch texture



Branches should connect from the planet to small environmental symbols.



Use:

- arrows

- leaves

- water drops

- lightbulb

- recycling symbol

- music notes

- roots

- sun

- small ecosystem drawings



Keep the illustration minimal and sophisticated.



Do NOT make it look childish.



The Mind Map should visually summarize the entire BlueGreen experience.



Animation:

- Planet appears first

- Branches draw outward one by one

- Icons appear subtly

- Lines animate as if being hand-drawn

- Very gentle motion only





09 — CONTACTS



Finish with a clean cream/off-white section.



Title:

“Contacts”



Keep this section minimal.



Use the BlueGreen visual language and subtle natural outline decoration.



Include placeholders for:

- Email

- Phone

- Social media

- Location



Do not invent contact information.





INTERACTION & MOTION:



The website should have subtle motion throughout.



Use:

- smooth scrolling

- fade-in animations

- gentle upward text reveals

- image parallax

- slow image zoom

- hand-drawn line animations

- subtle floating ecological illustrations

- section transitions



IMPORTANT:

Motion must remain subtle and premium.



Do NOT use:

- aggressive animations

- bouncing elements

- excessive transitions

- flashy gradients

- distracting hover effects

- excessive 3D effects



The website should feel cinematic and alive, but calm.



RESPONSIVE DESIGN:

Desktop:

- Large editorial compositions

- Large cinematic images

- Split layouts

- Generous whitespace



Tablet:

- Maintain visual hierarchy

- Adjust image/text proportions



Mobile:

- Stack image and text naturally

- Keep navigation clean

- Maintain the same visual identity

- Make the Mind Map readable and responsive

- Preserve the hand-drawn illustration style



TECHNICAL:

Build this as one long scrolling landing page.



Use semantic sections with IDs:

#hero

#journey

#play

#logic

#melody

#wonder

#story

#mindmap

#contact



Navigation links should smoothly scroll to their corresponding sections.



The BlueGreen logo on the LEFT must specifically scroll to #mindmap.



Make all images optimized for web performance while preserving high visual quality.



The final website should closely follow the composition and visual rhythm of the provided reference screenshot, while using the BlueGreen content and environmental imagery described above.



Overall experience:

A cinematic environmental journey — where nature, education, play, logic, music, transformation and storytelling gradually connect into one visual ecosystem.
